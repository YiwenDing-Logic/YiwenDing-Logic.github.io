Quantale-Enriched Algebras

Doctoral thesis · Submitted

Classical universal algebra reasons about equations. In my doctoral work, I study a broader setting in which algebras carry order or generalized distance, using categories enriched over a commutative quantale.

The thesis develops a common framework for these settings: it relates congruences to quotients, gives completeness results for graded inequational logic, and characterizes classes of algebras through enriched SP and HSP theorems.

Logic in Computer Science

My work in this area includes preservation and definability for the fluted fragment, van Benthem-style characterization theorems, polarity-based semantics for non-distributive modal logics, and game semantics for the lattice-based modal μ-calculus.

Modal Model Theory in Lean 4

Formalization · 2026

My Lean 4 project formalizes results from classical modal model theory, including van Benthem’s characterization theorem and Hennessy–Milner results. Supporting developments cover saturation and ultraproducts.

Built on mathlib and CSLib, the project brings logical characterization results into a machine-checked setting. The repository includes theorem-type checks, axiom audits, and continuous integration.
