\section*{Doctoral Thesis}
\textbf{Quantale-Enriched Algebras}\par
Develops a theory of algebras enriched over a commutative quantale, connecting classical universal algebra with ordered and quantitative settings. Using enriched category theory, the thesis establishes a congruence--quotient correspondence and proves Birkhoff-style and Pavelka-style completeness for a graded inequational logic. It proves enriched SP and HSP theorems characterizing classes of algebras definable by implications and inequations, and transports these results to separated algebras via separated reflection. These results provide a common approach to algebraic reasoning with equality, order, and generalized distance.

\section*{Formalization}
\textbf{\href{https://github.com/YiwenDing-Logic/modal-characterization-in-lean4}{Modal Characterization in Lean 4}}\hfill 2026\par
Lean 4 formalization of classical modal model theory, built on mathlib and CSLib. Includes van Benthem's characterization theorem for basic unimodal logic, modal definability of pointed model classes, bisimulation-somewhere characterizations, and Hennessy--Milner results, together with supporting saturation and ultraproduct constructions. The repository provides theorem-type checks, axiom audits, and continuous integration.
